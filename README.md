## 🎯 Business Objectives & Problem Statement
* **Siloed Reporting:** Warehouse physical volume and financial revenue were tracked across disconnected systems.
* **Workforce Visibility:** High-strain cold storage environments lacked isolated tracking against company illness thresholds.
* **Unit Economics:** Departmental performance needed evaluation on real handling efficiency (DKK / Tonne) rather than top-line revenue alone.

---

## 🔑 Key Metrics & Insights
* **Revenue & Volume Alignment:** Tracked **312.67M DKK** against a **312.30M DKK** target (**100.12% Target Attainment**) backed by **67,075 inbound tonnes**.
* **Workforce Stability Benchmark:** Monitored department-level sick leave against a **5.0% target cap**. Identified specific operational strain in the **Frost department (~5.5%)** due to sub-zero working conditions, providing an evidence base for targeted shift rotations.
* **Profitability & Yield:** Modeled a **182.07M DKK Net Margin**, identifying Meat as the highest yield driver (**6,190 DKK / Tonne**) and Fruits & Vegetables as the highest throughput volume driver (**~29.8K Tonnes**).

## 🛠️ Technical Stack & Implementation
* **Platform:** Power BI Desktop
* **DAX Formulas:** Dynamic target attainment, variance measures, yield per handled tonne, and net operational margin calculations.
* **Design System:** Dark Bento theme (`#1E293B` slate cards, `#334155` borders, 8px corner radius) with high-contrast accessibility and dynamic conditional threshold formatting.
